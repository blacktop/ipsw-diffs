en.FOOTER_TEXT_GENERATIVE_APPLEVISION = "Siri and Dictation\n\nIf you choose to enable Siri AI and opt in to Improve Siri \u0026 Apple Intelligence, Apple may store all of your interactions with Siri and Dictation, including the audio data and transcripts of what you say and type to Siri, and the response Siri provides.\n\nTo protect your privacy, this data is not associated with your Apple Account, and Apple takes steps to ensure personal details are not stored or used by Apple. For example, Apple does not collect the photos or attachments you provide to Siri, the content of notifications Siri reads to you, or the information Siri searches on your device. We also take steps to remove data such as phone numbers and email addresses, as well as content from messages, emails, or on-device search results used in Siri’s responses.\n\nAudio data, transcripts including requests, responses, and conversations, and related request data, such as the category of request you made, device specifications, performance statistics, and the approximate location of your device at the time the request was made, may be used to develop and improve Siri, Dictation, Search, and other language processing functionality in Apple products and services. For example, transcripts may be used to fine-tune Siri, Search, Voice Control, Translate, and automatic speech recognition models. Additionally, Siri audio data, transcripts, and related request data may be used to train Apple’s foundation models, which power Apple Intelligence and other Apple features, as well as features in apps that use Apple’s Foundation Models framework. A subset of this data may be reviewed by graders, with audio review being conducted only by Apple employees.\n\nYour Siri audio data, transcripts, and any related request data are associated with a random, device-generated identifier that rotates multiple times per hour and is not tied to your Apple Account or email address. To improve Siri’s ability to provide more personalized and relevant responses, Apple may connect multiple random identifiers associated with a conversation over time if you reengage with that conversation. If you make follow up requests to continue a conversation with Siri, Apple will also collect what you say to Siri and the responses provided in that conversation.\n\nData that Apple collects when you choose to opt in to Improve Siri \u0026 Apple Intelligence may be retained and used for up to two years, and the small subset of interactions that has been reviewed may be kept beyond two years for the ongoing improvement of Siri, Dictation, Search, other language processing features, and Apple’s foundation models.\n\nYour request history is not used to build a marketing profile, and is never sold to anyone.\n\nIf one of your Siri or Dictation interactions is selected for review, the request, as well as the response Siri provided, will be analyzed to determine accuracy and to generally improve Siri, Dictation, Search, other language processing functionality in Apple products and services, and Apple’s foundation models. Depending on the context of your request, a reviewer may access additional contextual data directly relevant to the request, such as names of your contacts or apps installed on your devices, in order to grade the effectiveness of Siri’s response. Only Apple employees, subject to strict confidentiality obligations, are able to access audio interactions with Siri and Dictation. You can change your setting for Improve Siri \u0026 Apple Intelligence at any time. To do so, go to Settings \u003e Privacy \u0026 Security \u003e Analytics \u0026 Improvements.\n\nChatGPT Extension\n\nWhen you choose to enable the ChatGPT extension in Siri and opt in to Improve Siri \u0026 Apple Intelligence, Apple may store all of your requests, including the audio recording, and transcripts of your request, and any conversation associated with that request. This data is part of your Siri and Dictation request history, and is used and retained by Apple as described above.\n\nApple collects limited information about ChatGPT’s response back to you, such as the approximate size of the response in order to operate the service and prevent fraud. For more information, visit www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/chatgpt-extension.\n\nYou can delete the transcripts of your Siri and Dictation requests that are stored on your device by going to Settings \u003e Siri \u003e Turn Off Siri and tapping Turn Off Siri.\n\nYou can disable Siri and Dictation by going to Settings \u003e General \u003e Keyboard and tapping to turn off Enable Dictation. Then go to Settings \u003e Siri \u003e Listen for and tap Off. The transcripts of your Siri and Dictation requests that are stored on your device and used to personalize your experience will be deleted if you disable Siri and Dictation.\n\nTranslate\n\nIf you opt in to Improve Siri \u0026 Apple Intelligence, your audio, text input, and transcripts of your Translate requests, associated with random, rotating, device-generated identifiers, may be stored and reviewed to develop and improve Siri, Dictation, Translate, other language processing functionality in Apple products and services, and Apple’s foundation models.\n\nYour Translate requests may be retained for up to two years to help Apple develop and improve Siri, Dictation, Translate, other language processing functionality, and Apple’s foundation models. For example, transcripts may be used to fine-tune Siri, Search, Voice Control, Translate, and automatic speech recognition models. If one of your Translate interactions is selected for review, the translation request, as well as the translation response provided, will be analyzed to determine accuracy. Only Apple employees, subject to strict confidentiality obligations, are able to access your Translate audio. The small subset of your Translate interactions that have been reviewed may be kept beyond two years for ongoing improvement of Siri, Dictation, Translate, and other language processing functionality in Apple products and services.\n\nGeneral\n\nThe opt-in preference you selected when you set up an iPhone or iPad will apply to any new Apple connected device or supported HomeKit accessory set up with that iPhone or iPad that has Siri turned on. You can change this later in the settings for each device.\n\nBy enabling Improve Siri \u0026 Apple Intelligence, you agree and consent to Apple’s and its subsidiaries’ and agents’ transmission, collection, maintenance, processing, and use of this information to provide and improve Siri, Dictation, Search, Translate, other language processing functionality in Apple products and services, and to train Apple’s foundation models, as described above. Apple may process and store this information with trusted third-party service providers. At all times, information collected by Apple from your use of Siri, Dictation, or Translate will be treated in accordance with Apple’s Privacy Policy, which can be found at www.apple.com/privacy."
